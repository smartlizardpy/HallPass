      How to play

      Find the security keycard, the maintenance fuse and the exit key, then get to the main entrance before the clock runs out.

      Once you hold two of them, a room that shouldn't exist opens somewhere in the school. The key is inside.

      The caretaker listens. Sprinting, doors and heavy breathing carry a long way; crouching barely makes a sound. If he sees you, run. He is fast at first but tires after a long chase: keep sprinting, break his line of sight, then hide.

      Every minute the building rearranges itself. Nothing you can see will change.

      With friends (main menu): share a room code, then work as a team. If he catches someone, he takes them to Room 000, and anyone who reaches them can let them out. Everyone has to get out. M mutes your microphone, V waves.

      Tools in the school: the teachers' SCHOOLNET computers (the login is on a sticky note somewhere in the same room) show the security cameras, can send him off to check an announcement, and say where things are. Vote in the election and your candidate's promise comes true. Whatever you can use shows up under Tools, top right.

      Before you play

      Photosensitivity warning. LAST BELL has flickering lights, brief flashes, screen shake and visual noise. A small number of people can have seizures triggered by flashing lights or patterns, even with no history of epilepsy.

      If you or anyone in your family has a history of seizures, or you feel dizzy, sick or uncomfortable while playing, stop and take a break.

      You can turn the effects down now, or later in Settings under Reduce flicker, noise and shake.

      Play with friends

      Up to four of you in the same school. If he catches someone, the others can let them out of Room 000. Everyone has to get out.
